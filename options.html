Domain Priority

Move results from domains you trust to the top of Google Search, and choose which sections Google shows. Changes save automatically.

Preferred domains

A domain also matches its subdomains, so wikipedia.org covers en.wikipedia.org, de.m.wikipedia.org, etc. Star (★) up to 3 domains in the toolbar popup to look for them when they have no result on the first page. Each starred domain that’s missing costs one extra Google search in the background; heavy use can bring up CAPTCHAs, and the extension stops looking in that tab if it sees one.

Results and buttons

Checked sections are shown on Google; unchecked ones are hidden. Each group’s heading checkbox shows or hides the whole group.

Sections are recognised by their English headings, so this works when Google’s interface language is English.
